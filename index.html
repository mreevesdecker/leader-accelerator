The Problem

The gap runs in both directions.

The view from your chair

You can see exactly how it should run. It just never left your head.

You’ve made a thousand calls on instinct, and that instinct built the company. But instinct doesn’t hand off. It doesn’t scale, and it can’t be delegated — so the business still routes through you.

The plan you keep meaning to write down is real. It’s just never made it out of your head and into something your team can hold.

How it shows up Your calendar fills with decisions that shouldn’t need you. You hold work you meant to hand off months ago. And stepping back feels impossible, because when you do, things stall.
The view from your team

They’d own it. Nobody ever showed them what owning it means.

They’re not checked out — they’re unclear. They can’t see the plan, so they can’t run it. They watch you decide fast and can’t reverse-engineer how you got there.

Without a clear picture of the outcomes, the decision rights, and the standard, “ownership” is a word said in meetings — not something anyone can actually do.

How it shows up Work waits on your yes. Standards get guessed at instead of met. Your best people stay busy but never fully take the wheel — and the ambitious ones start looking elsewhere.

“Great people with no system still can’t run the business without you. Build the system and equip the people to run it.”
